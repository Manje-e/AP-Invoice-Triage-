Invoice Approval and Payment Policy

Policy Number: FIN-POL-001 | Version: v2.1 | Effective Date: 01 January 2026
Approved By: CFO, Board of Directors | Classification: Internal — Confidential

---

1. Purpose

This policy establishes the framework for invoice approval, payment authorisation, and expenditure control at Investors Ltd. It ensures that all payments are legitimate, properly authorised, and compliant with internal financial controls.

---

2. Scope

This policy applies to all departments, employees, vendors, and contractors who submit or process invoices at Investors Ltd. It covers all invoice types including goods, services, consulting, and recurring contracts.

---

3. Definitions

Invoice: A document submitted by a vendor requesting payment for goods or services delivered to Investors Ltd.

Approver: The designated employee responsible for verifying and authorising an invoice before payment is processed.

Purchase Order (PO): An internal document issued by Investors Ltd authorising a vendor to supply goods or services up to a specified value.

Threshold: The maximum invoice amount that can be approved at each authorisation level without escalation.

---

4.1 Mandatory Fields — Missing Fields Flag

Every invoice submitted to Investors Ltd must contain the following mandatory fields. Any invoice missing one or more of these fields is flagged as MISSING_FIELDS and cannot be processed until corrected. Incomplete invoices must be returned to the vendor immediately.

Required fields:
- Vendor name and GST number
- Invoice number (unique per vendor)
- Invoice date and due date
- Amount in INR
- Department the invoice is raised against
- Name of the designated approver at Investors Ltd
- Description of goods or services provided
- Payment terms (Net 15, Net 30, or Net 45)

---

4.2 Currency

All invoices must be denominated in Indian Rupees (INR). Invoices in foreign currency must be converted to INR at the RBI reference rate on the invoice date before submission.

---

4.3 GST Compliance

All vendor invoices must be GST compliant. The vendor's GSTIN must be valid and active at the time of submission. Finance will verify GSTIN status before processing payment.

---

5.1 Standard Approval — Invoices Up To Rs.1,00,000

Invoices with a total value up to Rs.1,00,000 (one lakh rupees) may be approved by the designated departmental approver. The approver must be a confirmed employee of Investors Ltd at Manager level or above. No additional sign-off is required for invoices below this threshold.

---

5.2 Senior Approval — Threshold Breach Invoices Between Rs.1,00,001 and Rs.1,50,000

Invoices with an amount between Rs.1,00,001 and Rs.1,50,000 are classified as threshold breach invoices and are flagged as THRESHOLD_BREACH. These invoices exceed the standard approval limit and require senior approver sign-off in addition to the departmental approver.

Senior approvers are: Finance Manager, Operations Head, or equivalent Grade 6+ employees.

Payment cannot be processed until both the departmental approver and a senior approver have signed off. Target turnaround: 3 business days from receipt.

A threshold breach invoice is one whose amount falls in the range Rs.1,00,001 to Rs.1,50,000. This is a separate category from high value invoices which exceed Rs.1,50,000.

---

5.3 Board Approval — High Value Invoices Above Rs.1,50,000

Invoices exceeding Rs.1,50,000 (one lakh fifty thousand rupees) are classified as high value invoices and are flagged as HIGH_VALUE. High value invoices represent the highest risk category and require board-level authorisation before any payment is made.

A high value invoice is any single invoice whose amount is greater than Rs.1,50,000. This threshold is separate from the threshold breach range (Rs.1,00,001 to Rs.1,50,000).

Required documentation for high value invoices:
- Verified Purchase Order matching the invoice
- Vendor credential check (GST, PAN, bank account verification)
- Board resolution or written approval from two board members

Target turnaround for high value invoices: 7 business days from receipt. Finance must not process payment until all documentation is received and filed.

---

6.1 Standard Payment Terms

- Net 15: Payment due 15 days from invoice date
- Net 30: Payment due 30 days from invoice date
- Net 45: Payment due 45 days from invoice date

---

6.2 Due Soon — Priority Processing

Any invoice with a due date within 7 calendar days of today must be flagged as DUE_SOON and treated as priority. These invoices must be escalated immediately to the approver. Late payment penalties are the responsibility of the approver who failed to action the invoice in time.

---

6.3 Overdue Invoices

Invoices past their due date must be reported to the Finance Manager weekly. Repeated late payments to the same vendor may result in renegotiation of payment terms.

---

7.1 Return Policy — Missing Fields

Any invoice flagged as MISSING_FIELDS — meaning it is missing one or more mandatory fields as listed in Section 4.1 — must be rejected immediately by Finance, returned to the vendor with a written explanation of what is missing, and must not be entered into the payment system until corrected.

---

7.2 Responsibility

The departmental approver is responsible for ensuring invoices submitted by their vendors are complete before forwarding to Finance. Repeated submission of incomplete invoices by a vendor should be escalated to the Vendor Management team.

---

8.1 Round Amount Invoices — Suspicious Round Numbers

Invoices with an amount that is an exact multiple of Rs.1,00,000 — such as Rs.1,00,000, Rs.2,00,000, Rs.3,00,000 — are flagged as ROUND_AMOUNT and must be treated with additional scrutiny. Real invoices for goods and services rarely land on exact lakh amounts. A round amount invoice is not the same as a high value invoice. ROUND_AMOUNT is a separate flag type from HIGH_VALUE.

Round amount invoices may indicate estimated or fabricated billing and require:
- Supporting documents — itemised breakdown, delivery receipts, or timesheet
- The associated Purchase Order
- Independent verification of the amount before approving payment

---

9. Policy Violations

Any employee found to have approved an invoice in violation of this policy — including approving above their threshold, approving incomplete invoices, or bypassing controls — will be subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination. Finance reserves the right to reverse any payment made in violation of this policy.

---

10. Review and Amendments

This policy is reviewed annually by the CFO and Finance Manager. Any amendments require board approval before taking effect.
